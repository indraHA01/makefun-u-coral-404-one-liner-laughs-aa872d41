# My Love Life Is a 404 Error

An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-liner-laughs-aa872d41) bounty **"One-Liner Laughs"** — one funny line, at most 200 characters.

## The joke

> My love life is a 404 error — even Google can't find it, and Google found my embarrassing search history from 2011 in 0.03 seconds.

The angle: not the tired "I'm single" punchline, but the web's most famous dead end pointed at romance. Google is the search engine that finds *everything* — so when even Google can't find my love life, the setup is already a joke. The sting is the contrast: the same Google dug up my embarrassing search history from 2011 in 0.03 seconds. One setup, one twist, one specific number that makes it land.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 131 characters, one line) and validates it against the gate first: 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, single line.

```bash
python3 main.py
# writes /output/entry.txt (override with OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out)
```

No dependencies, no network, no secrets. MIT licensed.
